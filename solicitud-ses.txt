______________________________________________________________________________

Panamails is an email marketing platform for small and medium organizations in
Panama: savings and credit cooperatives, private schools, universities and
trade associations, most of them in the province of Veraguas. These
organizations already hold their own member and client lists. Panamails is the
tool they use to write to those people.

WHAT WE SEND TODAY
Transactional messages only. When someone creates an account on our panel at
panel.panamails.com we email them a six-digit confirmation code, and we send
account notices such as a warning to the owner of an address when somebody
tries to register with it. Current volume is a few dozen messages per month.

WHAT WE WILL SEND
Marketing campaigns on behalf of our customers, to lists those customers own.
We are the sending platform; the list belongs to the customer, who is
responsible for having obtained it lawfully. We expect fewer than 50,000
messages per month across all customers during the first year.

HOW RECIPIENTS OPT IN
The lists our customers upload are their own members, students or clients, who
gave the organization their address directly. Our terms of service
(https://panamails.com/terminos/, sections 5 and 8) require every customer to
declare that each address was given to them by its owner and that they can
prove it, and forbid purchased, rented, exchanged or scraped lists.

Sending is disabled by default. A new account can import contacts and draft
campaigns but cannot send anything until we contact the organization and enable
it by hand. No customer can start sending on their own.

HOW RECIPIENTS OPT OUT
Our platform injects a one-click unsubscribe link into every campaign at the
moment of sending. Customers cannot remove it, edit it or point it elsewhere,
because it is added by the sending pipeline and not by the customer's HTML. We
also set the List-Unsubscribe header.

An unsubscribe is immediate and permanent: it is written to a per-account
suppression list that the customer cannot delete, and every send checks that
list before the first message goes out. Anyone who writes to us asking to be
removed is suppressed manually the same day.

HOW WE HANDLE BOUNCES AND COMPLAINTS
Every message we attempt and every delivery event is recorded. Hard bounces and
complaints are added to the suppression list permanently and are never
contacted again; an unsubscribe can only be reversed if the person subscribes
again and confirms by email.

An account whose bounce rate goes above 3% or whose complaint rate goes above
0.05% is paused automatically and told why. New accounts send under a reduced
cap for their first 48 hours regardless of their plan. We are connecting SES
event notifications through SNS so suppression happens without human
intervention; while our volume is transactional only we also review the sending
log by hand every day.

AUTHENTICATION
The domain panamails.com is verified in SES with DKIM enabled, we use a custom
MAIL FROM domain (mail.panamails.com) and DMARC is published for the domain.

COMPLIANCE
We operate under Panama's Law 81 of 2019 on personal data protection. Campaign
emails will carry the sender's physical postal address, as CAN-SPAM requires
for recipients in the United States. Our privacy policy is published at
https://panamails.com/privacidad/ and describes exactly what we store, for how
long and with which providers.

CASILLAS DE CONFIRMACION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Marcalas todas. Las tres son ciertas en Panamails:

  - Solo se envia a quien pidio recibir esos correos: los terminos lo exigen
    al cliente y el envio no se habilita sin aprobacion manual.
  - Hay un proceso para rebotes y quejas: lista de supresion por cuenta, pausa
    automatica en 3% de rebotes y 0.05% de quejas.
  - Hay forma de darse de baja: enlace de un clic inyectado en cada campana,
    mas la cabecera List-Unsubscribe.

______________________________________________________________________________

Our customers do not buy or rent lists, and we do not provide lists of any
kind. Each customer uploads addresses that were given to their own
organization: members of a savings cooperative, parents of a private school,
alumni of a university, members of a trade association.

Before a customer can send anything, we speak with the organization and enable
sending by hand. During that conversation we ask where the list came from and
how the addresses were collected. An organization that cannot answer that does
not get enabled.

Every campaign carries a one-click unsubscribe link that the customer cannot
remove, and unsubscribes, hard bounces and complaints are suppressed
permanently per account and checked before every send. Accounts that exceed 3%
bounces or 0.05% complaints are paused automatically.
